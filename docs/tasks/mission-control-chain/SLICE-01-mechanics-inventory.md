## 2026-10-08 CreateCards retry control flow — stronger resolution
`BundleDataRoot.CreateCards` ISIL step 26 allocates a fresh item list and step 61 allocates a fresh card list. Step 71 calls AddCardItems, step 113 calls CreateCard, step 147 calls virtual ValidateCards, then steps 157-170 call Initialize and compare validated count with requested display count. On shortfall, step 170 jumps to step 32, which **recreates the item and card lists**. `BundleDataRoot.Initialize` restores runtime display count from configured +0x40 and resets the local initialized state. Therefore duplicates cause a whole-screen retry, not an in-place refill of surviving cards. For a five-card Blueprint-only reward whose items independently invoke GetRandomBlueprintData, the applicable exact distribution is iid weighted sampling *conditioned on five distinct Blueprint identities*, represented by `rejection_draft_target_probability`. Remaining checks: confirm special reward virtual validation override and the five-card asset's single Blueprint item configuration, then apply this distribution to Wave-60 chain simulation.
