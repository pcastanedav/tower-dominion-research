# Slice 02 — State and policy specification (draft)

## Scope and evidence status
This slice specifies a simulation interface without claiming that the unresolved five-card asset serialization is fully decoded. Mechanics already verified in Slice 01 are distinguished from explicit baseline assumptions. Slice 01 remains active until its eligibility checklist is complete; this document can progress independently.

## Run state
- `wave`: integer 1..60; transitions occur in wave order.
- `mc_in_hand`: nonnegative integer, Blueprint copies acquired but not yet built.
- `mc_built`: nonnegative integer, Mission Controls placed; track `mc_1a_active` separately if upgrade application can lag.
- `amplifier_in_hand`, `amplifier_built`, `amplifier_mc_targeted`: separate counters/flags; a reserved first Amplifier is not equivalent to an already activated MC-specific weight modifier.
- `mc_3a_processed`: number or set of built MC instances whose two special drafts have already fired; prevents retriggering on the same MC.
- `draft_queue`: finite work queue for pending MC 3A special drafts, preserving same-wave recursion.
- `placed_building_types`: set/multiset to account for building-doctrine pool pollution only after placement, not mere Blueprint ownership.
- `first_mc_wave`, `first_mc_built_wave`, `first_amplifier_wave`, `first_chain_threshold_wave`: separate observables.
- `red_copies`: ordinary reinforcement selected-Blueprint quantity rule; must not double offered slots or special-draft reward unless proven.

## Per-wave transition order (baseline proposal; exact in-game order to verify)
1. Update wave-dependent eligible Blueprint weights. Apply +2 tier-weight units per active MC 1A to eligible T2/T3 Blueprint base weights, before per-building-type percentage modifiers. Keep these dimensions distinct.
2. Produce ordinary reinforcement candidates (3 cards x 2 items; Tander 4 x 2) and applicable Eleanor periodic 1-of-3 Blueprint draft. Apply a selection policy that values MC and Amplifier from Wave 1. Ordinary Blueprint selection yields 2 copies for Red, otherwise 1; special reward quantity is not presumed doubled.
3. Add selected Blueprint copies to inventory. Reserve first Amplifier for MC even if MC is not yet available; build/activate it when feasible. Build MCs as soon as economic assumptions permit, and apply 1A promptly.
4. Before Wave 40, model MC 1A activation without automatic MC 3A reward; from Wave 40 onward, assume new MC can be upgraded immediately to 3A and enqueue exactly two five-choice drafts.
5. Drain the special draft queue. For each five-choice offer, choose MC when present. If a new MC copy is obtained, build and upgrade it immediately when permitted, enqueuing two new drafts. Each MC instance triggers at most once. The process terminates when the queue empties or an explicit finite safety limit is reached; reaching the limit is reported as censored, never silently counted as an infinite chain.
6. Record wave-end inventory and threshold metrics; do not interpret Blueprint ownership as proof of actual placement when space limits are unverified.

## Selection policy and counterfactuals
- Baseline: pursue MC and Amplifier concurrently starting Wave 1. First Amplifier is reserved for MC; subsequent Amplifier allocation is conditional on chain maturity. MC is selected whenever offered in an eligible choice, subject to choosing between MC and Amplifier in the same reinforcement screen; that conflict requires an explicit policy (proposed priority: first MC unless Amplifier already owned, with sensitivity alternative).
- Candidate choice is by **card**, not independent item slot: choosing a card grants its contents; mixed MC+Amplifier card may give both. Preserve card grouping and any opportunity cost of the unchosen cards.
- Comparison policies: no Amplifier pursuit; Amplifier-first in conflicting screens; delayed Amplifier; Wave 35/40/45 3A affordability.
- Threshold sensitivity: combined MC owned (`in_hand + built`) >= 2/3/4/5, with a separate built-only threshold metric.

## Unresolved mechanics and modeling safeguards
- Exact serialized `SpecificBlueprints` list for Among5: use the rejection-law sampler only in an explicitly labeled conditional scenario until confirmed.
- Determine whether a duplicate five-card screen is wholly retried and whether all five independently sample unrestricted Blueprint pool (strong evidence, not fully proven together).
- Confirm eligible Blueprint roster, faction/commander combinations, and Amplifier building-type/rarity; do not fabricate them.
- Validate timing and count of Eleanor periodic drafts, 1A activation, and upgrade cost thresholds.
- Construction space/placement limits and second-Amplifier policy are not proven; keep configurable and report their sensitivity.
- Chain explosion is a branching process with possible supercritical behavior; cap work and record censoring rather than assuming guaranteed termination.

## Required output metrics
P(first MC by Wave 5/10/15), median first-MC wave, P(owned-MC threshold by Wave 40/45/50/60), wave-end MC owned/built distributions at Waves 10/20/35/40/45/50/60, Amplifier activation timing, recursion-trigger counts, and censored-run fraction. Report standard errors/intervals and random seeds for stochastic results.
