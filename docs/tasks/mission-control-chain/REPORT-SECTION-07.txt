SECTION 7 — AMPLIFIER ANALYSIS
Status: draft for editorial review.
Source: SIMULATION-1000-RUNS.json (current comparison); earlier 500-run sensitivity batch clearly marked.

7.1. How Amplifiers affect Blueprint acquisition

Each Amplifier assigned to Mission Control adds +100% to its Blueprint rarity weight. Bonuses stack additively:

0 Amplifiers: 1x weight
1 Amplifier: 2x weight
2 Amplifiers: 3x weight

Rarity weight is not the same as acquisition probability. Mission Control's probability depends on the combined weights of eligible Blueprints, including competing Tier 2 and Tier 3 Blueprints affected by Mission Control's 1A upgrade. The effect therefore depends on the current reward pool.

7.2. Natural Amplifier acquisition

Two independent Doctrines grant Amplifiers:
Tier I D1_45: one Amplifier.
Tier II D2_42: another Amplifier.

Both can be acquired naturally, but neither is guaranteed. Early acquisition allows the bonus to affect more later Blueprint opportunities.

An Amplifier acquired before the first Mission Control becomes useful when a Mission Control is available to receive its effect.

The current reward-selection policy prioritizes an initial Amplifier but does not systematically seek a second after obtaining the first. This is a model limitation, not a game mechanic.

7.3. Guaranteed Tier I Doctrine from Wave 1

The alternative condition begins with D1_45 already owned at Wave 1, removes it from later Doctrine rewards, and leaves D2_42 naturally obtainable.

The maximum remains two Amplifiers, for a 3x weight multiplier.

This supersedes the earlier artificial saved-Amplifier scenario, which applied a permanent additional weight bonus on top of naturally acquired Amplifiers.

7.6. Why Magel responds differently

Magel's additional choose-one-of-three Doctrine draft every other wave creates more opportunities to encounter Amplifier-granting Doctrines naturally.

AVERAGE MC COUNT — MAGEL
Checkpoint      Natural   Tier I from W1    Change
Wave 40           2.822           2.959     +0.137
Wave 60          10.290          10.472     +0.182
Wave 63          12.093          12.315     +0.222
Wave 103         46.167          46.586     +0.419

These modest differences are consistent with the hypothesis that Magel already has relatively good natural access to Amplifiers.

However, the current results do not separately measure the probability or timing of each Doctrine's acquisition. This is an interpretation, not an isolated causal measurement.

7.7. Amplifier sensitivity and model limitations

An earlier 500-run sensitivity experiment gave a Baseline Wave-60 mean of 3.182 MCs with Amplifier effects disabled, compared with 7.172 in the earlier natural-acquisition batch.

These are prior-batch figures, not directly matched 1,000-run results, and must not be conflated with the current comparison.

A future controlled analysis could compare zero, one, and two Amplifiers under the same 1,000 seeds and current model implementation, recording when each Amplifier is obtained and applied.

Until then, distinguish the measured effect of guaranteeing Tier I access from the broader causal effect of Amplifiers.

SECTION 7 SUMMARY

Amplifiers increase Mission Control's Blueprint rarity weight and can accelerate chain growth.

Guaranteeing the Tier I Doctrine from Wave 1 improves average acquisition in all five current scenarios. Lions gains the most MCs on average at Wave 63, Eleonor gains the most at the 10-MC threshold, and Magel has the smallest incremental gain.

These results measure guaranteed early Tier I access, not the isolated effect of each individual Amplifier or its acquisition timing.
