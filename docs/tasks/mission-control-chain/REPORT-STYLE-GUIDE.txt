TOWER DOMINION — MISSION CONTROL CHAIN REPORT
PDF STYLE GUIDE — APPROVED DESIGN DIRECTION

1. VISUAL IDENTITY
Technical strategy publication with restrained military science-fiction styling.
A4 portrait. Light analytical pages on warm ivory (#F4F2EC), with dark navy (#172331) headings, slate (#26394C) supporting elements, muted gold (#D5A34B) highlights, and steel gray (#6B7885) secondary text.
Only the cover should use a full dark background. Avoid ornamental clutter.

2. TYPOGRAPHY AND PAGE GEOMETRY
Modern readable sans serif (Source Sans 3 or Liberation Sans, subject to availability and embedding rights).
Body approximately 10.5–11 pt, comfortable line spacing, margins approximately 20 mm with a slightly wider inner margin.
Consistent header, footer, section hierarchy, page numbering, and navigable PDF bookmarks.
Major sections begin on a new page; subsections flow naturally.

3. SCENARIO COLORS
Baseline #777777 (reference).
Lions #D64545 (faction).
Eleonor #36965C (commander).
Tander #3478D4 (commander).
Magel #9B59B6 (commander).
Use colors consistently for scenario identification in charts and tables, not arbitrary decoration.

4. TABLES AND NUMBERS
Right-align numeric values. Means: two decimal places in report presentation; probabilities: one decimal place; probability differences: percentage points (pp).
Subtle horizontal separators rather than heavy grids.
Place simulation conditions and sample sizes with table titles.
Repeat column headers on continued pages. Avoid splitting short tables.
Do not imply greater precision than the underlying results support.

5. CHARTS
Charts must be generated from SIMULATION-1000-RUNS.json, never approximated from sketches.
Suggested figures: MC growth by recorded checkpoint, Wave 63 acquisition thresholds, natural versus Tier I-from-Wave-1 comparison, Eleonor versus Magel late-game comparison.
Use vector artwork when possible; legends and labels must be readable.
Straight connections between recorded checkpoints are visual guides, not interpolated simulation outputs.

6. EVIDENCE AND INTERPRETATION
Distinguish simulation results from interpretations. Interpretive notes may use a thin gold rule and neutral background.
Limitations use subdued gray. Do not imply that acquisition probabilities are survival probabilities.
Preserve precise distinction between Lions (faction) and Eleonor, Tander, Magel (commanders).

7. COVER AND NAVIGATION
Dark navy typography-first cover with report title, subtitle, and study scope.
Compact contents page with clickable links and PDF bookmarks for sections and appendix.
Number interior pages; omit or separately style cover numbering.

8. PAGINATION AND CONTENT INTEGRITY — MANDATORY
Never leave a label, heading, title, caption, or introductory line isolated at the bottom of a page while its associated content starts on the next page.

- Keep each section/subsection heading with at least the first two lines of following text.
- Keep table title, subtitle, column headers, and at least first data row together.
- Keep chart title, chart, legend, and figure caption together whenever the figure fits on one page.
- Keep numbered-item labels and explanatory labels with the content they introduce.
- If the required group does not fit, move it to the next page as a unit.
- Use paragraph widow/orphan control: avoid single lines stranded at the top or bottom of pages.
- Long prose and long tables may break at logical boundaries to avoid excessive blank space; repeat table headers and preserve context.
- Avoid splitting compact comparisons or callouts.
- Review actual rendered page boundaries; automatic keep-together behavior alone is not sufficient.

9. FINAL OUTPUT VALIDATION
Searchable/selectable text, embedded fonts when permitted, crisp vector graphics, no clipped text, no collisions with margins or footers, and consistent terminology.
Inspect every rendered page, especially page breaks, table continuations, chart captions, and isolated labels.
Do not generate the final PDF until the text source has completed editorial review.
